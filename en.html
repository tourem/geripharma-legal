Geripharma is a remote medical follow-up mobile application for patients supported by a healthcare professional. It lets you track your medication intake, record health measurements, store medical documents and take part in video consultations with your doctor.

This policy explains what personal data we process, why, who it is shared with, and what your rights are. It applies to the Geripharma mobile application (com.geripharma.app) on Android and iOS, wherever you live.

2. Which law applies?

As Gerimassy Consulting SRL is established in Belgium, all processing falls under the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), pursuant to its Article 3(1), whatever your country of residence. The rights and safeguards described in this policy therefore apply to you identically, whether you live in Europe or in Africa.

In addition to that framework, and without replacing it, the national data protection laws of your country of residence also apply. For patients residing in Mali, this means Law No. 2013-015 of 21 May 2013, as amended, on the protection of personal data in the Republic of Mali, enforced by the Personal Data Protection Authority (APDP). Where the two frameworks differ, we apply the more protective one.

6. Where is your data hosted and transferred?

Hosting. The server application, the database and your medical documents are hosted on Google Cloud Platform, in the europe-west1 region (Belgium, European Union). Your health data is not stored outside the European Union.

Transfers outside the European Union. Some of our processors handle data from countries outside the European Union for which the European Commission has issued no adequacy decision. This is the case for providers established in Mali (text message delivery, payment processing) and, for certain technical operations, for providers established in the United States. These transfers are governed by the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission (Article 46(2)(c) GDPR), supplemented where necessary by additional technical and organisational measures. The data transferred is limited to what the service requires: a phone number and the content of a notification for text messages; a subscription reference and a transaction status for payment. No medical document and no health record is transferred to these providers.

You may obtain a copy of the safeguards in place by writing to the contact address given in section 1.

Transfer from Mali to Belgium. For patients residing in Mali, storing data on our servers constitutes a transfer outside Mali within the meaning of Article 11 of Law No. 2013-015 of 21 May 2013, as amended. That transfer relies on the contractual safeguards agreed with our host, including the standard contractual clauses, and on the fact that the recipient is established in the European Union and subject to the GDPR, which ensures a high level of protection of privacy and fundamental rights.

8. What are your rights?

You have the following rights over your data:

  • Access — obtain a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data or complete it
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data
  • Restriction — request that processing be temporarily suspended
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format, or have it transmitted to another controller
  • Objection — object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on our legitimate interest
  • Withdrawal of consent — at any time, where processing relies on your consent
  • Post-mortem instructions — determine what happens to your data after your death, where the applicable law provides for this

Limit on the right to erasure. Your medical record cannot be erased on request during the period stated in section 7. That retention is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims within the meaning of Article 17(3)(e) GDPR, having regard in particular to the medical record retention obligations applicable in the country where care is delivered. During that period the record is no longer used to provide you with the service: it is retained, and nothing more.

To exercise these rights, write to minthe@geripharmaapp.com. We respond within one month, reduced to thirty days for rectification requests falling under Article 13 of the Malian law. 9. How to delete your account

You can delete your account directly from the application:

Profile → Privacy and security → Close my Geripharma account

This immediately deactivates your account: you no longer have access to the application and the service ends. Your medical record is not erased at the same moment, for the reasons set out in section 8.

Your medical record is then retained for ten years from the last activity. At the end of that period, your data is permanently erased.

You may also write to minthe@geripharmaapp.com to request deletion of your account and data.

10. How do we protect your data?

  • All communication between the application and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS and secure WebSocket).
  • Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Access to your account relies on cryptographically signed authentication tokens with a limited lifetime.
  • Your medical documents are not publicly exposed: they are served through restricted signed links that expire after fifteen minutes.
  • Technical secrets for the service are held in a managed vault (Google Secret Manager).
  • Access to data is limited to those who need it as part of your care.
  • Everyone with access to the data is bound by a duty of confidentiality.

11. In the event of a data breach

If a data breach occurred and posed a risk to your rights and freedoms, we would notify the competent supervisory authority within the statutory time limits. If the risk were high, we would also inform you directly and without undue delay.

12. Minors

Geripharma is intended for adult patients followed by a healthcare professional. Under the user agreement, registration is reserved for people of legal age, or accompanied by their legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from minors outside that framework. If you believe a minor has provided us with data outside that framework, write to us and we will delete it.
