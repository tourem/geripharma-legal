1. Purpose and scope

Geripharma is a mobile application for remote geriatric and medical follow-up, intended for patients supported by a healthcare professional. It lets you track medication intake, record health measurements, store medical documents and take part in video consultations.

We are committed to protecting our users' privacy, and health data in particular, which is sensitive data. This policy sets out what data is collected, why, how it is secured, who it is shared with, and what your rights are.

It applies to the Geripharma mobile application (com.geripharma.app) on Android and iOS, to the connected devices associated with it, and to the services delivered by our servers, wherever you live.

3. Which law applies?

As Gerimassy Consulting SRL is established in Belgium, all processing falls under the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), pursuant to its Article 3(1), whatever your country of residence. Belgian health data legislation also applies, in particular the Act of 30 July 2018 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and the Act of 22 April 2019 on the quality of healthcare practice.

The rights and safeguards described in this policy apply to you identically, whether you live in Europe or in Africa. In addition to that foundation, and without replacing it, the national data protection laws of your country of residence also apply. For patients residing in Mali, this means Law No. 2013-015 of 21 May 2013, as amended, on the protection of personal data in the Republic of Mali, enforced by the Personal Data Protection Authority (APDP). Equivalent laws apply in the other West African countries where the service is offered. Where these frameworks differ, we apply the more protective one.

7. Hosting, sovereignty and transfers

Location. All health data is hosted on Google Cloud Platform, in the europe-west1 region (Belgium, European Union). The server application, the database and your medical documents are stored there. Your health data is not stored outside the European Union.

Transfers outside the European Union. Some of our processors handle data from countries outside the European Union for which the European Commission has issued no adequacy decision: providers established in Mali (text message delivery, payment processing) and, for certain technical operations, providers established in the United States. These transfers are governed by the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission (Article 46(2)(c) GDPR), supplemented where necessary by additional technical and organisational measures. The data transferred is limited to what the service requires: a phone number and the content of a notification for text messages; a subscription reference and a transaction status for payment. No medical document and no health record is transferred to these providers.

Transfers from West African countries. For patients residing there, storing data on our servers constitutes a transfer outside the country of residence under the applicable national laws. For Mali, that transfer falls under Article 11 of Law No. 2013-015 of 21 May 2013, as amended: it relies on the contractual safeguards agreed with our host, including the standard contractual clauses, and on the fact that the recipient is established in the European Union and subject to the GDPR, which ensures a high level of protection of privacy and fundamental rights. Equivalent safeguards are in place for the other countries where the service is offered, in coordination with the competent national regulators.

You may obtain a copy of the safeguards in place by writing to the address given in section 2.

8. How do we protect your data?

  • Health data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS and secure WebSocket) and at rest in our databases.
  • Access to your account relies on cryptographically signed authentication tokens with a limited lifetime.
  • Your medical documents are not publicly exposed: they are served through restricted signed links that expire after fifteen minutes.
  • Technical secrets for the service are held in a managed vault (Google Secret Manager).
  • Access to patient information is strictly limited to authorised healthcare professionals and to the people you have explicitly authorised.
  • Everyone with access to the data is bound by a duty of confidentiality.

10. What are your rights?

You have the following rights over your data:

  • Access — consult all recorded data, scores and readings, and obtain a copy
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data or complete it
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data, subject to the statutory medical record retention obligations
  • Restriction — request that processing be temporarily suspended
  • Portability — receive your health data in a structured, machine-readable format, or have it transmitted to another controller
  • Objection — object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to any non-essential processing based on our legitimate interest
  • Withdrawal of consent — at any time, where processing relies on your consent
  • Post-mortem instructions — determine what happens to your data after your death, where the applicable law provides for this

These rights may be exercised by you or, where your condition warrants it, by your legal representative or the person of trust you have designated.

Limit on the right to erasure. Your health record cannot be erased on request during the archiving period stated in section 9. That retention answers a legal obligation within the meaning of Article 17(3)(b) GDPR as regards Belgian law, and in any event remains necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims within the meaning of Article 17(3)(e). During that archiving period the record is no longer used to provide you with the service: it is retained, and nothing more.

To exercise these rights, write to minthe@geripharmaapp.com or use the support area of the application. We respond within one month, reduced to thirty days for rectification requests falling under Article 13 of the Malian law. We may ask you for proof of identity where we have reasonable doubt as to the identity of the requester. Exercising these rights is free of charge.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority:

11. How to delete your account

You can delete your account directly from the application:

Profile → Privacy and security → Close my Geripharma account

This immediately deactivates your account: you no longer have access to the application and the service ends. Your account and profile data is deleted or anonymised within a maximum of 30 days.

Your health record is not erased at the same moment: it is archived for the period stated in section 9, for the reasons set out in section 10. At the end of that period, your data is permanently erased.

You may also write to minthe@geripharmaapp.com to request deletion of your account and data.

12. In the event of a data breach

If a data breach occurred and posed a risk to your rights and freedoms, we would notify the competent supervisory authority within the statutory time limits. If the risk were high, we would also inform you directly and without undue delay.

13. Minors and protected persons

Geripharma is intended for adult patients followed by a healthcare professional. Under the user agreement, registration is reserved for people of legal age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with data, write to us and we will delete it.

Where the patient is unable to express their wishes, the account may be opened and administered by their legal representative or by the person of trust they have designated, under the conditions provided by the applicable law. That person acts in the patient's interest and is bound by the same confidentiality rules.
