Geripharma is a remote medical follow-up mobile application for patients supported by a healthcare professional. It lets you track your medication intake, record health measurements, store medical documents and take part in video consultations with your doctor.

This policy explains what personal data we process, why, who it is shared with, and what your rights are. It applies to the Geripharma mobile application (com.geripharma.app) on Android and iOS.

8. How to delete your account

You can delete your account directly from the application:

Profile → Privacy and security → Close my Geripharma account

This immediately deactivates your account: you no longer have access to the application and the service ends. Your medical record is not erased at the same moment, as it is subject to a retention obligation.

Your medical record is then retained for a defined period: [[ retention period for the medical record and the corresponding statutory obligation ]]. At the end of that period, your data is permanently erased.

You may also write to [[ contact email address ]] to request deletion of your account and data.

9. How do we protect your data?

  • All communication between the application and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS and secure WebSocket).
  • Access to your account relies on cryptographically signed authentication tokens with a limited lifetime.
  • Your medical documents are not publicly exposed: they are served through restricted signed links that expire after fifteen minutes.
  • Technical secrets for the service are held in a managed vault (Google Secret Manager).
  • Access to data is limited to those who need it as part of your care.

10. Minors

Geripharma is intended for adult patients followed by a healthcare professional. Under the user agreement, registration is reserved for people of legal age, or accompanied by their legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from minors outside that framework.
