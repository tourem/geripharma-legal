This page explains how to request deletion of your Geripharma account (com.geripharma.app) and what happens to your data. It is published by Gerimassy Consulting SRL, the data controller.

1. From the application

Profile → Privacy and security → Close my Geripharma account

You are asked to confirm. This immediately deactivates your account: you no longer have access to the application and the service ends.

2. By e-mail

If you no longer have access to the application, write to minthe@geripharmaapp.com from the address associated with your account, requesting deletion of your account and data. We reply within one month.

3. What is deleted

DataPeriod
Account, identification and profileDeleted or anonymised within a maximum of 30 days
Contacts of your relativesDeleted when the account is closed
Server logs, crash reports, e-mail delivery events12 months

4. What is retained, and why

Your health record is not erased at the same moment. It is archived for 30 years from the last contact, not exceeding 50 years, in accordance with the patient record retention obligation set out in article 35 of the Belgian Act of 22 April 2019 on the quality of healthcare practice.

Subscription and billing data is retained for 10 years under applicable accounting law.

At the end of those periods, your data is permanently erased.

This retention is a legal obligation: it cannot be waived at your request, and it prevails over the right to erasure for the part of the record it covers.

5. Further information

Details of the data processed, the recipients and your rights are set out in our privacy policy.
